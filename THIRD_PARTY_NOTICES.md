## Redistributed material

Before a tagged public release, exact dependency versions and required
copyright/license texts must be reviewed.

Where third-party files are redistributed directly, their required notices
should be placed under:

`third_party/licenses/`

Whenever possible, Open Cloud Assistant should install upstream software from
its official distribution source instead of vendoring entire third-party
source trees.
